Papers Posts Research Experience

I am a final-year Ph.D. candidate at MMLab, CUHK, advised by Hongsheng Li and Xiaogang Wang, and currently a Research Intern at ByteDance Seed working with Haoqi Fan. My research focuses on generative models, unified multimodal models, world models, and agentic systems.

Previously, I interned at Reve (with Han Zhang) and Google DeepMind (with Long Zhao), and collaborated with Avolution AI (acquired by MiniMax; with Zhaoyang Huang) and Tencent AI Lab. Across these roles, I worked on generative and multimodal modeling, including Data-Space Iteration, Representation Forcing, Omni, ConsistencySolver, PromptRL, Rectified Diffusion, Diffusion-NPO, Phased Consistency Model (adopted in Qwen-Image-2512 and FastVideo), and AnimateLCM (1M+ Hugging Face downloads), alongside earlier open-source continual learning (PyCIL, 1k+ GitHub stars).

I enjoy unpacking the math and systems behind modern models from first principles—writing deep-dive notes on distributional & trajectory distillation, RL / OPD / VSD density ratios, and long-context attention & KV-cache kernels. I want to stay curious, chase what excites me, and live wholeheartedly.
